# How To Add/Remove Global Hierarchies Filters For Recommendations

This guide explains how to add or remove hierarchies in the Global Filters for your Recommendations.

To do that, perform the following steps:

1.  In your Dashboard, go to **Recommendations** and click **Overview**;
2.  Click the **Recommendation** you want to configure;_This image shows steps 1 and 2: navigating to Recommendations in the left menu under ON-SITE and opening the Recommendations overview._
3.  On that page, scroll down to **Recommendation Strategy** and expand the section;
4.  Click **Add filter**. In the first input, select "hierarchies". In the second input, choose the operator. In the third input, select the hierarchies to include. To add more hierarchies, click **Add filter** again and repeat the selections;

     _This video shows steps 3 and 4: expanding Recommendation Strategy to access Filters and configure them._

To remove some hierarchies from your global filters, perform the same steps as above, but set the second input to "matches none of" in the **Recommendation Strategy** section;Once the filter is created, click **Save and publish** to activate your changes.

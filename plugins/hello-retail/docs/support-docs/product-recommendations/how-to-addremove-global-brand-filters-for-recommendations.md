# How To Add/Remove Global Brand Filters For Recommendations

This guide explains how to add or remove brands in the global filters for your Recommendations. Follow these steps:

1.  On your Dashboard, navigate to **Recommendations** and click **Overview**;
2.  Click the **Recommendation** you want to add a global brand filter for;_This image shows steps 1 and 2 in the Dashboard Overview, where a user navigates to Recommendations under the On-site section._
3.  On that page, scroll down to **Recommendation Strategy** and expand the section;
4.  Click **Add filter**. In the first field, select brand. In the second field, choose the operator. In the third field, select the brand. To add more brands, click **Add filter** again and repeat the steps for each brand.

_This video shows steps 3 and 4, where the user expands Recommendation Strategy to view and customize Filters._

To remove a brand from your global filters, follow the same steps you use to add one. In the second field of the **Recommendation Strategy** filter, select does not match to exclude the brand.

Once the filter is configured, click **Save and publish** to activate it.

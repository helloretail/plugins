# How to Edit Titles on Product Recommendations

This guide explains how to change the headline of a Product Recommendation box and update any visible labels inside the box.

**Step 1:** [Log in to your Hello Retail Business account](https://my.helloretail.com/company/signin.html).

**Step 2:** Go to **Recommendations** > **Overview**, then click the Product Recommendation box you want to edit.

**Step 3:** In the editor for that Product Recommendation box, open **Design**. Update the headline and any label text as needed.

When you are done, click **Save** to apply the changes.

# Signup & Login

This guide covers how to get invited to Hello Retail and how to log in to the Dashboard.

* * *

## Signup

Signup requires an invitation from an account owner or admin on an existing Hello Retail account.

You can read about how to add users to an account [here](how-to-add-users-to-your-hello-retail-account.md).

* * *

## Login Dashboard

1.  Go to: [https://my.helloretail.com/company/signin.html](https://my.helloretail.com/company/signin.html)
2.  Enter the email address and password associated with your Hello Retail user.
3.  Select Sign in to open the Dashboard.

* * *

If you have any questions or need more info, [contact us here](https://helloretail.com/contact-us/) or [request a demo of the complete Hello Retail solution here](https://helloretail.com/demo) before you sign up.

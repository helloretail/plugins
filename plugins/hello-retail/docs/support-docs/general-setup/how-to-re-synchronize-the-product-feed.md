# How to Re-Synchronize the Product Feed

## How to Re-Synchronize the product feed

This guide explains how to manually re-synchronize your product feed.

* * *

Manually updating your product feed lets you import recent changes immediately, such as price updates, availability, or attribute changes.

This is useful during peak campaigns such as **Singles Day**, **Black Friday** and **Cyber Monday**.

* * *

The following steps show how to re-synchronize or update your product feed manually:

1. Start by logging into your Hello Retail account. On the dashboard view, navigate to the left-hand side menu. Under the **Configuration** section, click on **Data Setup** to unfold the drop-down menu.
2. Next, from the drop-down menu, click on **Overview.**
3. From the Data Setup Overview window, click on **Product data.** When the box unfolds, click on **See feeds.**
4. Click on the **Re-synchronize products now** button.
5. This queues a resynchronization job and shows a progress indicator. When the indicator completes, the latest feed content has been imported. Processing time depends on catalog size.

# FAQs

## What is Hello Retail?

Hello Retail provides personalization products for e-commerce. You can use them individually or together to personalize the full customer journey. Products include Recommendations, personalized Newsletter Content, Search, Triggered Emails such as price drop or abandoned cart, and Pages.

These products help increase sales performance, differentiate your brand, and improve customer engagement.

Find more [details about all Hello Retail products here](hello-retail-features.md).

## How does Hello Retail work?

With your permission, Hello Retail collects on-site behavioral data during visits. We analyze this data to build individual affinity profiles for your webshop visitors. These profiles drive personalized content across Recommendations, Search, Triggered Emails, and Pages, so each visitor experiences a tailored site.

You can [read more about Hello Retail's personalization here](hello-retail-personalization-explained.md).

## How much does it cost?

Because we tailor each implementation, pricing depends on your setup and requirements. Contact our sales team for an estimate. They will help scope the right solution for your business and provide a quote.

You can [reach out to us here](https://helloretail.com/contact/) or [book a demo](https://helloretail.com/demo/).

## Is it easy to install?

Yes. Installing Hello Retail on your webshop is straightforward. You add a small JavaScript snippet to your site template or tag manager, and then configure the features you want to use.

Find [more details on the installation here](installing-the-javascript.md).

## Do I need to have a lot of technical knowledge to use it?

No. Our onboarding specialists guide you through installation, configuration, and ongoing maintenance. Support is included.

You decide how the products should work and look on your webshop. We handle the implementation and ensure it follows best practices and any preferences you specify.

If you have in-house developers, you can also implement and customize the setup yourself. Our products are designed to be easy to integrate.

## I don’t like other companies adding scripts to my website. How safe is it to use Hello Retail?

We process data only to deliver the features you configure and for the purposes you agree to. See our customer references for how we work with data responsibly.

You can read more about the Hello Retail [cookies](../other/about-the-hello-retail-cookies.md) and [JavaScript here](about-the-hello-retail-javascript.md).

## Can I decide how the features work and look?

Yes. All features are configurable and can be adapted to your business goals and brand. You can define placement, design, and business rules.

If you want us to make changes, contact us and we will implement them for you.

## What webshop hosts are compatible with Hello Retail?

Hello Retail works with any e-commerce platform that allows you to add our JavaScript snippet and expose required data feeds or APIs.

[Here you can find a few examples](https://support.helloretail.com/platforms-and-newsletter-providers/setup-of-platforms-category/) of how to get started on platforms like [Magento](../platforms-and-newsletter-providers/magento-1-installation-guide.md), [Shopify](../platforms-and-newsletter-providers/shopify-installation-guide.md), [WooCommerce](../platforms-and-newsletter-providers/woocommerce-installation-guide.md), [PrestaShop](../platforms-and-newsletter-providers/prestashop-installation-guide.md), [SmartWeb](../platforms-and-newsletter-providers/smartweb-installation-guide.md), [DanDomain](../platforms-and-newsletter-providers/dandomain-installation-guide.md), [Shoporama](../platforms-and-newsletter-providers/shoporama-installation-guide.md), [E37](../platforms-and-newsletter-providers/e37-installation-guide.md) and more.

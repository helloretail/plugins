# Recommendations on Data Tracking & Permission Handling

Security and protection of personal data online are receiving increased attention. Cookie tracking, email permissions, anti-spam rules, and related regulations are core topics for technical e-commerce teams.

At Hello Retail, we work with these topics daily. We continuously update our guidance so customers can run effective e-commerce within legal and best-practice boundaries.

As a Hello Retail customer, you can choose a managed service package with an assigned account manager. Your manager will help plan and implement Hello Retail solutions for conversion optimization, including guidance on configuring our tools in compliance with the laws in your markets.

Some customers may not be fully up to date on local rules. This section collects practical information, links to official resources, and Hello Retail best practices on data tracking and permissions. We update this page when relevant changes occur. Check back regularly.

Always validate requirements for your jurisdictions. We do not receive automatic legal updates, and this page may not include the latest or complete set of rules. If in doubt, contact our support.

You, as the e-commerce merchant, are responsible for data processing on your site. This includes cookie disclosures, consent collection and storage, and ensuring that your chosen third parties handle data and tracking in compliance with applicable laws.

# About the Hello Retail Cookies

When you use Hello Retail, you must comply with local regulations and include cookie information in your privacy policy.

Use the following recommended language for your privacy policy. It lists the cookies and session storage keys that Hello Retail sets in the browser.

**Service**: Hello Retail
**Purpose**: Collects information about users and their activity on the website. The data is used to track and analyze behavior and to deliver product recommendations, product merchandising, and features for an optimized user experience.
**Data processor privacy policy:** Hello Retail ([helloretail.com](https://helloretail.com))
**Provider**: [helloretail.com](https://helloretail.com)

-   aw\_source (session cookie)
-   aw\_target (session cookie)
-   awtracking\_optout (cookie, ~10 years)
-   hr\_initial\_load (optional session cookie)
-   aw-{domain}\_cc (session storage)
-   aw-{domain}-{\*}\_cc (session storage)
-   aw-{domain}\_cchash (session storage)
-   aw-{domain}-{\*}\_cchash (session storage)
-   aw\_pb (session storage)
-   hello\_retail\_id (cookie, 365 days)

**Service**: Hello Retail
**Purpose**: Collects information to improve the on-site search experience. Applies only when Hello Retail Search is implemented.
**Data processor privacy policy:** Hello Retail ([helloretail.com](https://helloretail.com))
**Provider**: [helloretail.com](https://helloretail.com)

-   search\_search\_redirects (session storage)
-   search\_search\_redirects\_hash (session storage)
-   search\_input\_template\_{\*} (session storage)
-   search\_initialization\_code\_{\*} (session storage)
-   search\_result\_styles\_{\*} (session storage)
-   search\_input\_hash\_{\*} (session storage)
-   search\_initialization\_hash\_{\*} (session storage)
-   search\_style\_hash\_{\*} (session storage)

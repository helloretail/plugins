# Setting up the Post-Conversion Trigger

In this guide, you can read and learn about how to set up the post-conversion trigger for triggered emails.

* * *

## [Steps on How to Set Up the Post-Conversion Trigger](back-in-stock-trigger-design.md#steps-on-how-to-set-up-the-back-in-stock-trigger-design)

1.  Start by logging into your Hello Retail account. On the dashboard, go to the left-hand menu. Under **Emails**, click **Triggered Emails** to open the drop-down menu.
2.  In the drop-down, click **Post-conversion**.
3.  On the **Post-conversion** page, click **Create new trigger**.

    In cases where you want multiple post-conversion triggers active:

    You can enable up to three post-conversion triggers and set the delay between each trigger.
4.  Tick the **Enable trigger** checkbox. Specify when the email should be sent. You can also choose whether to send the post-conversion email if the customer places another order before the scheduled send.

5.  Specify the email subject line and select the design you want.

6.  Select the number of related products to display and configure the product selection rules.

7.  Configure the permission settings. Your already uploaded permissions will be used. This option may be subject to local regulations. Ensure compliance with the rules in your country. **Save** your changes – the Post-conversion trigger is now ready.

> **Note:**
>
> To preview the email, send a test email to your inbox from the trigger editor.

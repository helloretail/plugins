# Auto Sync of Get A Newsletter permissions

This guide explains how to automatically synchronise subscriber permissions from your Get A Newsletter account to Hello Retail.

**Step 1:** Generate an **API token** in your Get A Newsletter account.

Log in to your Get A Newsletter account and navigate to: **Contacts** > **API & Forms**.

Inside **API & Forms** go to the section **API tokens** and click "Add token".

Enter the name "Hello Retail API" and an optional description, then click "Save".

Copy the **API token** so you can enter it in your Hello Retail account. Click "Save" to confirm.

_If you prefer a text guide to finding your API token, see:_ [https://support.getanewsletter.com/en/articles/3678438-generate-an-api-token](https://support.getanewsletter.com/en/articles/3678438-generate-an-api-token)

**Step 2:** In your Hello Retail account, go to **Data Setup** > **Permissions**, then click the **Set up automatic permission synchronisation** button:

**Step 3:** In the setup dialog:

1. select Get A Newsletter in the **Type** drop-down;
2. paste your Get A Newsletter API token into the **API token** field;
3. select the list from your Get A Newsletter account that you want to sync in the **List** drop-down:

Remember to click **Save**.

**Step 4:** Hello Retail will now synchronise permissions from the selected Get A Newsletter list automatically on a daily basis.

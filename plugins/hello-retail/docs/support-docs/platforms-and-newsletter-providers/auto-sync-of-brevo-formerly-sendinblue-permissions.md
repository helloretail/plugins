# Auto Sync of Brevo (formerly SendinBlue) permissions

This guide explains how to automatically synchronise permissions from your Brevo account to Hello Retail.

**Step 1:** First, find or generate an **API key** in your Brevo account. Follow the guide below:

Log in to your Brevo account and navigate to: **Account (Username, Top Right Corner)** > **SMTP & API**.

Inside **SMTP & API**, click **GENERATE A NEW API KEY** in the top right corner.

Insert the name of the **API Key** as **Hello Retail API**, ensure Version **3.0** is selected, and press **GENERATE**.

Copy the **API Key** you just generated named **Hello Retail API** so you can enter it into your Hello Retail account later.

**Step 2:** Once you have your Brevo **API Key**, navigate to **Triggered Emails** > **Permissions**, then click the **Set up automatic permission synchronisation** button within your Hello Retail account:

**Step 3:** As the next step, you'll have to:

1.  select SendinBlue (this is the previous name for Brevo) in the **Type** drop-down
2.  paste your Brevo **API Key** into the **API Key** field
3.  select the list from your SendinBlue account you would like to sync in the **List** drop-down

Click **Save**.

**Step 4:** Setup is complete. Your SendinBlue list permissions will now be synchronised automatically on a daily basis.

# How to Integrate Recommendations into your Newsletters

This guide shows how to add Hello Retail product recommendations to your newsletters. It also includes short video tutorials for popular newsletter providers.

* * *

## Newsletter Integrations

Getting started with Hello Retail product recommendations in newsletters takes a few steps:
1. Create your first Newsletter Content campaign in Hello Retail Business. See the guide: [how to set up your first campaign in Hello Retail Business admin](../newsletter-content/get-started-with-newsletter-content.md).
2. Copy the generated HTML snippet for the campaign.
3. Paste the snippet into your email template in your newsletter platform, then preview and test-send.

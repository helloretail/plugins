# Integrate Newsletter Content into MailCamp

This guide explains how to integrate Hello Retail Newsletter Content with MailCamp.

**Before you start:**

- You need a design for your newsletter content. If you do not already have a design for your newsletter, you can follow [this guide](../newsletter-content/create-a-newsletter-content-design.md).
- If you already have a design, create an Auto Campaign configuration by following [this guide](../newsletter-content/set-up-an-auto-campaign-configuration.md).

You are now ready to integrate your newsletter content into MailCamp.

## How to integrate newsletter content into MailCamp

**Step 1:** Log in to your Hello Retail account and copy the code snippet from your Auto Campaign configuration.

**Step 2:** Log in to your MailCamp account and open the campaign where you want to display Hello Retail Newsletter Content.

> **Note:**
>
> Images can appear broken in the MailCamp preview. Send a test email to verify that images render correctly.

**Step 3:** Insert the code snippet from Hello Retail into your newsletter template, as shown in the video guide.

Remember to click "Save".

**Congratulations**!

You have now successfully integrated Hello Retail Newsletter Content into your newsletters.

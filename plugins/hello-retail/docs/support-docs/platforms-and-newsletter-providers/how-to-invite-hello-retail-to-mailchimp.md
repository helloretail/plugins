# How to Invite Hello Retail to MailChimp

If you need help configuring Hello Retail in your Mailchimp account, you can grant us access by adding us as a user.

### Video Guide

First, log in to your Mailchimp account at [https://login.mailchimp.com/](https://login.mailchimp.com/)

### Text Guide

First, log in to your Mailchimp account at [https://login.mailchimp.com/](https://login.mailchimp.com/)

**Step 1:** Click the avatar with your company name in the top-right corner, then select **Account**.

**Step 2:** Click **Settings > Users**.

**Step 3:** Click **Invite a User**.

****

**Step 4:** Invite [support@helloretail.com](mailto:support@helloretail.com) and set **User Type** to **Manager** or **Admin**.

# Auto Sync of Campaign Monitor permissions

Use this guide to set up automatic synchronisation of permissions from your Campaign Monitor account.

**Step 1:** Generate or locate your API key in Campaign Monitor.

Log in to your Campaign Monitor account and navigate to: **Account Settings** > **API Keys.**

Save your **API key** and **Client ID** so you can enter them in your Hello Retail account later.

_If you want to see a text-guide for how to find your API-Key and Client ID you can find it here:_ [https://help.campaignmonitor.com/api-keys](https://help.campaignmonitor.com/api-keys)

**Step 2:** Once you have your Campaign Monitor API key, navigate to **Data Setup** > **Permissions**, then click the **Set up automatic permission synchronisation** button:

**Step 3:** As the next step, you'll have to:

1.  Select Campaign Monitor in the **Type** drop-down.
2.  Paste your Campaign Monitor API key into the **API-Key** field.
3.  Select the list from your Campaign Monitor account you want to synchronise in the **List** drop-down:

    Remember to click **Save**.

**Step 4:** Setup is complete. Your Campaign Monitor list permissions will now be synchronised automatically on a daily basis.

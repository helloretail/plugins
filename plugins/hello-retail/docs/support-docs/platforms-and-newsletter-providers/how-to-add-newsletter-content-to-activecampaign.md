# How to Add Newsletter Content to ActiveCampaign

> **Note:**
>
> Complete the steps in [Get started with Newsletter Content](../newsletter-content/get-started-with-newsletter-content.md) before using this guide.
>
> This guide assumes you are using ActiveCampaign built-in email templates.
>
> The Hello Retail code snippet is optimized for emails with a 650 px width.

**Step 1**: In your ActiveCampaign account, open the campaign where you want to show your Hello Retail product recommendations.

_(Click the “Campaigns” tab in the left menu)_

_(Click “Continue” to open the Campaign Editor)_

**Step 2**: In the Campaign Editor, drag the “&lt;/&gt; HTML Block” from the right menu into your email template.

_(Drag the &lt;/&gt; HTML Block into your email template)_

**Step 3**: Copy the `<code snippet>` with product recommendations from your Hello Retail account and paste it into the “&lt;/&gt; HTML Block” as shown below.

 _(Insert the Hello Retail code into the &lt;/&gt; HTML Block and press “Save”)_

**Step 4**: Your Hello Retail product recommendations are now embedded in your email template.

> **Note:**
>
> To change how many products appear per row on desktop, adjust the width value in the snippet (for example, width="205"). Higher values show fewer products per row. Lower values show more.

For example, width="300" shows 2 products per row.

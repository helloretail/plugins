# Auto Sync of Bullsender Permissions

This guide explains how to automatically synchronize your subscriber permissions between Hello Retail and Bullsender.

* * *

## Steps

1.  Log in to your Bullsender admin account.

2.  In the dashboard, open the left-hand menu and go to **User Settings**.

3.  On the User Settings page, locate the API key field and copy the key.

4.  Log in to your Hello Retail account. In the left-hand menu, under the **Configuration** section, click **Data Setup** to open the drop-down.

5.  From the drop-down, click **Permissions** and then **Set up automatic permission synchronization**.

6.  Select **Bullsender** as the type, paste the copied API key into the **Key** field, and choose the **List** you want to synchronize with Hello Retail.

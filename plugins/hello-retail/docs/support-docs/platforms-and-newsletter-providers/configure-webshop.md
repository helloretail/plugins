# Configure Webshop

Use this guide to configure your webshop in Hello Retail after onboarding.

* * *

## Introduction

After you complete the Hello Retail onboarding flow, configure your webshop details as the final step.

First finish installing your platform integration and complete onboarding. Then complete the webshop configuration.

## Process

1.  Log in to your Hello Retail account.
2.  In the Dashboard, click the _Configure_ button.

3.  In the configuration view, enter your webshop details.
4.  Click _Save_ to apply your changes.

# How to export permissions from Mailchimp

This guide explains how to export email marketing permissions from Mailchimp. Use the CSV to synchronize with Hello Retail as part of enabling Triggered Emails.

1.  [Log in to Mailchimp here](https://login.mailchimp.com/).

2.  Navigate to **Lists** and select your list.

3.  Choose **Export List**.

4.  Mailchimp generates the CSV file.

    When the export is ready, click **Export As CSV** to download.

You are now ready to [synchronize your permissions with Triggered Emails](../triggered-emails/synchronizing-permissions.md).

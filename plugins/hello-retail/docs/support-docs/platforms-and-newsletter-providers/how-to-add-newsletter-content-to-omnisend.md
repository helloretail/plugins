# How to Add Newsletter Content to Omnisend

Here's a simple guide on how to add your newsletter content code snippet to [Omnisend](https://www.omnisend.com/).

> **Note:**
>
> Do not send more than one newsletter on the same day. Each campaign or broadcast name must be unique. Hello Retail uses the date as a unique ID for products in newsletters, and duplicates can occur if names or dates are reused.

#### Prerequisite

Before you can create an auto campaign, you must create a newsletter content design. To learn how, click [here](../newsletter-content/create-a-newsletter-content-design.md). When your design is ready, proceed with this guide to integrate with Omnisend.

### How to get your Hello Retail code snippet

**Step 1**: Log in to your Hello Retail account [here](https://my.helloretail.com/company/signin.html) and create an auto campaign for the product recommendations you want in the newsletter. _(If you already created a design, skip to Step 4.)_

**Step 2**: When logged in, navigate to: **Newsletter Content > Auto campaigns.**

- If you already have an Auto campaign config, click the **Edit auto campaign config** button.
- If you do not have an Auto campaign config, follow the steps below.

_(Click "Create new auto campaign config" to create a design and code snippet)_

**Step 3**: Fill out the fields. Choose either **2** or **3 products** per row from the drop-down, and select how many products to show in total. _(In this example, we show three products per row and six products in total in the newsletter.)_

_(Fill out the fields as shown)_

**Step 4:** Optionally select fixed products, which will always be shown, and set a **Recommendation strategy** (or keep the default). Then click the **Code snippet** tab, select **Omnisend** as the Newsletter platform, and copy the **code snippet** shown.

_(Select Omnisend as newsletter platform and copy the code snippet as shown)_

**Step 5**: You are now done in the Hello Retail account and ready to insert the newsletter content into your Omnisend email template.

### How to insert the Hello Retail code snippet into Omnisend

**Step 1**: Log in to your Omnisend account at [https://app.omnisend.com/#/login/](https://app.omnisend.com/#/login/).

**Step 2**: In the admin, go to **Campaigns** and locate the newsletter where you want to insert the newsletter content.

**Step 3:** Click **Edit** for an existing campaign, or click **+New campaign** to create a new newsletter.

****

**Step 4**: Follow the video guide below to implement the code snippet into an email template.

**Step 5:** After adding the code snippet, click **Next** in the top-right to proceed with sending the campaign, or click **Save & Exit** to send later.

**Step 6:** Send a test email to verify that rendering and links work as intended before sending to your audience.

# MailerLite Permission Guide

This guide explains how to set up automatic permission synchronization between your MailerLite account and Hello Retail.

* * *

## Getting the API Key from MailerLite

1.  Log in to your MailerLite account [here](https://app.mailerlite.com/users/login/).

2.  In the top right, click your profile avatar and select **Integrations**.

3.  On the **Integrations** page, open the **Developer API** integration. This takes you to the page where your **API Key** is shown.
4.  Click **Copy** to copy the **API Key**.

With your **MailerLite API Key**, proceed to add it in your Hello Retail account to enable permission synchronization.

* * *

## Inserting the MailerLite API Key into your Hello Retail Account

1.  Log in to your Hello Retail account. In the dashboard, open the left-hand menu. Under Configuration, click Data Setup to expand the drop-down.
2.  From the drop-down, click Permission campaigns. Then click **Edit**.

3.  On the Automatic permission synchronization page, configure the following:

    Select **MailerLite** in the **Type** drop-down.

    Paste the MailerLite **API-Key** into the **API-Key** field.

    Select the **List** from your MailerLite account that you want to synchronize.

4.  Click **Save**.

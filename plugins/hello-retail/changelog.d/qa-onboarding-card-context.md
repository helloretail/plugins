### Changed

- `search-qa`, `recom-qa`, `pages-qa`, `retailmedia-qa`, `newsletter-qa` and `qa-checklists` now also look for the customer's other Onboarding cards by customer / brand name, not only the card in hand, and ask the operator before using context from another domain of the same customer. On a yes they read that onboarding in full, carry over its decisions and declined or waived items, re-verify its open items, and never inherit URLs, logins, feeds, UUIDs or locale; the domain's own card always wins.
- When a build is a copy of another domain, the same skills ask the operator, then read the source domain's onboarding card and earlier QA files to learn why the design is as it is. A copy source from another customer contributes build facts only, never that customer's decisions, and a behaviour inherited from the source is never passed as by-spec unless this domain's own card says so.
- The report's ticket-context line records which sibling or copy source was used, or that it was declined.
